For few‑bit quantization, Zhu et al. Our Variational Network Quantization (VNQ) combines the Bayesian sparsity framework with an explicit multi‑modal quantizing prior, directly steering low‑variance weights toward the target quantization values. To compute the KL divergence term under this prior, we exploit the analytic approximation derived by Molchanov et al. [7] for a zero‑centered log‑uniform prior, extending it to a mixture of shifted priors that model multiple quantization levels. This integration allows VNQ to simultaneously prune and quantize in a single training objective, without the need for post‑hoc fine‑tuning.
