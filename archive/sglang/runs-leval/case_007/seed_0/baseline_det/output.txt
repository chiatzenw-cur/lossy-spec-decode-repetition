We conduct the first large‑scale empirical study of conventional regularization techniques—L1/L2 weight decay, weight clipping, dropout, batch‑normalization, and entropy regularization—on four state‑of‑the‑art policy‑optimization algorithms (A2C, TRPO, PPO, SAC) across nine continuous‑control benchmarks (MuJoCo and RoboSchool).
