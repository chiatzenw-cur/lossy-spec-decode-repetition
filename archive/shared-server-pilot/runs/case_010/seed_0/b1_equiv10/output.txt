\(\{1,2,\ldots ,10\}\).  
The number of possible draws is  

\[
\binom{10}{4}=210 .
\]

---

### Event \(B\): Grand prize  

Grand prize occurs when \(L=J\).  
Only one of the 210 possible draws equals \(J\):

\[
P(B)=\frac{1}{210}.
\]

---

### Event \(A\): Prize  

A prize is won if at least two of her numbers are in the drawn set, i.e.  
\(|J\cap L|\ge 2\).  
Count the draws that give exactly \(k\) matches:

\[
\#\{L:|J\cap L|=k\}= \binom{4}{k}\binom{6}{4-k}\qquad (k=0,1,2,3,4).
\]

Computing:

\[
\begin{aligned}
k=2 &: \binom{4}{2}\binom{6}{2}=6\cdot15=90\\[1mm]
k=3 &: \binom{4}{3}\binom{6}{1}=4\cdot 6=24\\[1mm]
k=4 &: \binom{4}{4}\binom{6}{0}=1\cdot1=1
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence

\[
P(A)=\frac{90+24+1}{210}=\frac{115}{210}=\frac{23}{42}.
\]

---

### Conditional probability

\[
P(B\,|\,A)=\frac{P(B)}{P(A)}=\frac{\dfrac{1}{210}}
{\dfrac{115}{210}}=\frac{1}{115}.
\]

Thus the desired probability is \(\displaystyle \frac{1}{115}\).
