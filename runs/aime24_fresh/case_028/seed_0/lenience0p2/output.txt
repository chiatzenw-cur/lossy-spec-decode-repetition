### 1.  Congruence conditions

Using \(1000\equiv 6\pmod 7\), \(100\equiv 2\pmod 7\) and \(10\equiv 3\pmod 7\) we obtain  

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{Change thousand digit:}&6&+2b+3c+d\equiv0 \pmod 7,\\
&\text{Change hundred digit:}&6a+2+3c+d\equiv0 \pmod 7,\\
&\text{Change ten digit:}&6a+2b+3+d\equiv0 \pmod 7,\\
&\text{Change unit digit:}&6a+2b+3c+1\equiv0 \pmod 7.
\end{aligned}
\]

Let’s denote the residues modulo \(7\) as \(A=a\bmod7,\;B=b\bmod7,\;C=c\bmod7,\;D=d\bmod7\).

From the first three equations we obtain  

\[
\begin{aligned}
D &\equiv 1-2B+4C,\\
D &\equiv A-2+4C,\\
D &\equiv A-2B+4.
\end{aligned}
\]

Equating the first two gives \(A\equiv 3-2B\),  
equating the first and third gives \(A\equiv4C-3\),  
equating the last two gives \(B\equiv 3-2C\).

Finally, the fourth condition yields  

\[
A \equiv 2B+3C+1 \pmod 7 .
\]

Substituting \(A=3-2B\) and \(B=3-2C\) gives
\[
3-2B \equiv 2B+3C+1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
5C-3\equiv0\pmod7\Longrightarrow C\equiv2\pmod7.
\]

Thus  

\[
C\equiv2,\qquad B\equiv3-2C\equiv6,\qquad A\equiv4C-3\equiv5\pmod7.
