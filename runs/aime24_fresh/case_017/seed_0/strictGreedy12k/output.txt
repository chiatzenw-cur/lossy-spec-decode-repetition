### 1.  Distance \(OI\)

For any triangle  

\[
OI^{2}=R(R-2r).
\]

Therefore  

\[
OI^{2}=13(13-12)=13,\qquad OI=\sqrt{13}.
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------

### 2.  Length \(IA\)

In \(\triangle OIA\) we know \(OA=R=13,\; OI=\sqrt{13}\) and \(OI\perp IA\).
Hence the right triangle \(OIA\) has hypotenuse \(13\):

\[
IA^{2}=OA^{2}-OI^{2}=13^{2}-13=156,\qquad IA=\sqrt{156}=2\sqrt{39}.
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------

### 3.  Semiperimeter information

For the incenter–vertex distance

\[
IA^{2}=r^{2}+(s-a)^{2}.
\]

Hence  

\[
156=36+(s-a)^{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad (s-a)^{2}=120
      \quad\Longrightarrow\quad s-a=2\sqrt{30}\;(>0),
\]
so  

\[
s=a+2\sqrt{30}. \tag{1}
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------

### 4.  Angle \(A\)

Using \(IA=r/\sin \tfrac{A}{2}\) (right triangle \(AID\) with \(ID=r\) and  
\(AD=s-a\)) gives

\[
\frac{IA}{r}= \frac{1}{\sin \tfrac{A}{2}}
            \;\Longrightarrow\;
\sin^{2}\frac{A}{2}=\frac{r^{2}}{IA^{2}}
          =\frac{36}{156}=\frac{3}{13}.
\]

Thus  

\[
\sin\frac{A}{2}=\sqrt{\frac{3}{13}},\qquad
\cos\frac{A}{2}=\sqrt{1-\frac{3}{13}}=\sqrt{\frac{10}{13}}.
\]

Hence  

\[
\sin A=2\sin\frac{A}{2}\cos\frac{A}{2}
       =2\sqrt{\frac{3}{13}}\sqrt{\frac{10}{13}}
       =\frac{2\sqrt{30}}{13}. \tag{2}
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------

### 5.  Side \(a=BC\)

From the sine law \(a=2R\sin A\):

\[
a=26\cdot\frac{2\sqrt{30}}{13}=4\sqrt{30}. \tag{3}
\]

Using (1) we find  

\[
s=a+2\sqrt{30}=4\sqrt{30}+2\sqrt{30}=6\sqrt{30}. \tag{4}
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------

### 6.  Area and product \(bc\)

The area of \(\triangle ABC\) can be expressed in two ways.

*Via the inradius:*  

\[
\Delta=rs=6\cdot6\sqrt{30}=36\sqrt{30}.
\]

*Via two adjacent sides and the included angle \(A\):*  

\[
\Delta=\tfrac12\,bc\sin A
      \;\Longrightarrow\;
bc=\frac{2\Delta}{\sin A}
   =\frac{2(36\sqrt{30})}{\dfrac{2\sqrt{30}}{13}}
   =468.
\]

Alternatively, using \( \Delta=\dfrac{abc}{4R}= \dfrac{abc}{52}\) and the
known \(a=4\sqrt{30}\) gives the same result:
\(bc=\dfrac{312s}{a}=\dfrac{312(6\sqrt{30})}{4\sqrt{30}}=468\).
