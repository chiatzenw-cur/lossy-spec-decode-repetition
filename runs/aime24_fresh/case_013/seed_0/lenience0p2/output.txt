### 1.  Substitute \(z\)

\[
\begin{aligned}
(75+117i)z 
&= (75+117i)\cdot 4e^{i\theta}
= (300+468i)\,e^{i\theta},\\[4pt]
\frac{96+144i}{z}
&= \frac{96+144i}{4}\,e^{-i\theta}
= (24+36i)\,e^{-i\theta}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence the expression becomes

\[
(300+468i)\,e^{i\theta} + (24+36i)\,e^{-i\theta}.
\]

---

### 2.  Compute the real part

Recall \(e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta\) and \(e^{-i\theta}=\cos\theta-i\sin\theta\).

\[
\begin{aligned}
(300+468i)(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)
  &= 300\cos\theta-468\sin\theta
    + i\bigl(468\cos\theta+300\sin\theta\bigr),\\[2pt]
(24+36i)(\cos\theta-i\sin\theta)
  &= 24\cos\theta+36\sin\theta
    + i\bigl(-24\sin\theta+36\cos\theta\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]

Adding the two real parts gives

\[
\boxed{R(\theta)=324\cos\theta-432\sin\theta }.
\]

---

### 3.  Maximize \(R(\theta)\)

Write \(R(\theta)=A\cos\theta+B\sin\theta\) with \(A=324,\;B=-432\).
For any real \(\theta\),

\[
A\cos\theta+B\sin\theta
  =\sqrt{A^2+B^2}\;\cos(\theta+\phi)
\]

where \(\phi\) satisfies \(\cos\phi=\tfrac{A}{\sqrt{A^2+B^2}}\) and
\(\sin\phi=\tfrac{B}{\sqrt{A^2+B^2}}\).

Compute \(A^2+B^2\):

\[
324^2+432^2 = 104\,976 + 186\,624 = 291\,600.
\]

Hence

\[
\sqrt{A^2+B^2} = \sqrt{291\,600}=540.
\]

Thus

\[
R(\theta) = 540\cos(\theta+\phi).
\]

The maximum of \(\cos(\theta+\phi)\) is \(1\), therefore

\[
\boxed{R_{\max}=540}.
\]
