\]

**Step 3 –  Area of one face**

For a triangle of side \(a,b,c\)

\[
\text{Area}= \frac14\sqrt{\,4a^{2}b^{2}-(a^{2}+b^{2}-c^{2})^{2}\,}.
\]

Using \(a^{2}=41,\;b^{2}=80,\;c^{2}=89\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
4a^{2}b^{2} &=4(41)(80)=13\,120,\\
(a^{2}+b^{2}-c^{2}) &=41+80-89=32,\\
\text{Area}^{2} &=\frac{13\,120-32^{2}}{16}= \frac{12\,096}{16}=756 .
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence

\[
\text{Area}=\sqrt{756}= \sqrt{36\cdot21}=6\sqrt{21}.
\]

All four faces are congruent, so

\[
\text{total area}=4\times 6\sqrt{21}=24\sqrt{21}.
\]

---

**Step 4 –  Inradius**

The inscribed sphere’s radius \(r\) satisfies

\[
V=\frac{r\,(\text{total surface area})}{3}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
r=\frac{3V}{\text{total surface area}} .
\]

Insert the computed expressions:

\[
r=\frac{3\,(160/3)}{24\sqrt{21}}
  =\frac{160}{24\sqrt{21}}
  =\frac{20}{3\sqrt{21}}
  =\frac{20\sqrt{21}}{63}.
\]

---

**Step 5 –  Expressing \(r\) as \(\dfrac{m\sqrt{n}}{p}\)**

The distance is \( \displaystyle \frac{20\sqrt{21}}{63}\).

Here  
\(m=20,\;n=21,\;p=63\).

They are relatively prime and \(21\) is square‑free (\(21=3\times7\)).

Therefore

\[
m+n+p=20+21+63=104.
\]
