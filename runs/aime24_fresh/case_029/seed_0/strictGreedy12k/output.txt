### 1.  Tangency condition

Let a point on the torus be parametrised by a tube angle \(\varphi\):

\[
x=(6+3\cos\varphi)\cos\theta ,\qquad
y=(6+3\cos\varphi)\sin\theta ,\qquad
z=3\sin\varphi .
\]

Only the tube angle \(\varphi\) matters for the distance to the sphere centre.
The squared distance from this point to \((0,0,h)\) is  

\[
d^2(\varphi)=\bigl(6+3\cos\varphi\bigr)^2+\bigl(3\sin\varphi-h\bigr)^2 .
\]

For the torus surface to be tangent to \(S\) the surfaces must have the same
normal vector at the touching point.  
Computing the gradients gives after simplification

\[
\tan \varphi =-\frac{h}{6}\, .
\]

Hence \(\varphi\) takes only two values, differing by \(\pi\); the corresponding
two tangency circles are symmetric with respect to the
\(z=0\) plane.

### 2.  Determining \(h\)

Let \(c=\cos\varphi\).  
From \(\tan\varphi=-h/6\) and \(c^2+\tan^2\varphi\,c^2=1\) we find

\[
c=\frac{\pm 6}{\sqrt{\,36+h^2\,}} .
\]

Insert \(c\) into the distance formula:

\[
d^2=\bigl(6+3c\bigr)^2+\bigl(3\sqrt{1-c^2}-h\bigr)^2=121 .
\]

With \(c\) as above one obtains after simplification

\[
\sqrt{36+h^2}\,\Bigl(1\pm\frac{3}{\sqrt{36+h^2}}\Bigr)=11 .
\]

Thus either

\[
\sqrt{36+h^2}=8   \quad\text{or}\quad \sqrt{36+h^2}=14 .
\]

The two corresponding values of \(h\) are

\[
h_1=\sqrt{28}=2\sqrt7 ,\qquad
h_2=4\sqrt{10}.
\]

Both describe a sphere that touches the torus along two circles.

---

### 3.  Radii of the circles

For a given \(\varphi\) the circle of intersection lies in the horizontal
plane \(z=3\sin\varphi\) and has radius in the \(xy\)-plane

\[
\rho = 6+3\cos\varphi
     =6\!\left(1\pm\frac{3}{\sqrt{36+h^2}}\right).
\]

Using the two values of \(\sqrt{36+h^2}\) we obtain

\[
\rho_1=\;6\!\left(1-\frac{3}{8}\right)=\frac{33}{7},
\qquad
\rho_2=\;6\!\left(1+\frac{3}{8}\right)=\frac{33}{4}.
\]

Thus  

\[
r_i=\frac{33}{7}\quad(\text{inner circle}),\qquad
r_o=\frac{33}{4}\quad(\text{outer circle}).
\]

---

### 4.  Difference

\[
r_o-r_i=\frac{33}{4}-\frac{33}{7}
      =\frac{33(7-4)}{28}
      =\frac{99}{28}.
\]

The fraction is already in lowest terms.  
Hence \(m=99,\;n=28\) and

\[
\boxed{m+n=127}.
\]
