Using the hypergeometric distribution, for \(k=0,1,2,3,4\):

\[
P(k)=\frac{\binom{4}{k}\binom{6}{4-k}}{\binom{10}{4}}.
\]

The probability of the grand prize (all four match) is

\[
P(4)=\frac{\binom{4}{4}\binom{6}{0}}{210}=\frac{1}{210}.
\]

The probability of winning any prize (at least two matches) is

\[
\begin{aligned}
P(k\ge 2) &= P(2)+P(3)+P(4)\\
&= \frac{\binom{4}{2}\binom{6}{2}+\binom{4}{3}\binom{6}{1}+\binom{4}{4}\binom{6}{0}}{210}\\
&= \frac{90+24+1}{210}\\
&= \frac{115}{210}\\
&= \frac{23}{42}.
\end{aligned}
\]

\frac{P(4)}{P(k\ge 2)}
