\[

\[

\]

### 2.  Split residents by how many of \(D,G,S\) they own

Let  

* \(x_1\) = residents that own exactly **one** of \(\{D,G,S\}\)  \(\;=\; 437\)  
* \(x_2\) = residents that own exactly **two** of \(\{D,G,S\}\)  \(\;=\; 234\)

---

### 3.  Relate the totals

A resident who owns exactly \(k\) of \(\{D,G,S\}\) contributes \(k\) to the total in (1).  
Therefore

\[
x_1 \cdot 1 \;+\; x_2 \cdot 2 \;+\; x_3 \cdot 3 \;=\; 1124 .
\]

\[
