### 1.  Count the total “attribute‑pair” incidences

Each resident has a number of attributes equal to the number of sets he/she belongs to.  
The total number of attribute‑pair incidences is

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{# of diamonds rings} + \text{# of golf clubs} + \text{# of garden spades} + \text{# of candy hearts} \\

\end{aligned}

\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total} &= 1\,d + 2\,c + 3\,b + 4\,a \\
&= d + 2(437) + 3(234) + 4a \\
&= d + 874 + 702 + 4a \\

Set this equal to 2024:

\[
d + 4a + 1576 = 2024 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad d + 4a = 448 .
