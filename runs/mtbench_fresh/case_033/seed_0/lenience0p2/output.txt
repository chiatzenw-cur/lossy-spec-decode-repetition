### Step‑by‑step

1. **Define the events**  
   - \(B\) = student likes **blue**  
   - \(G\) = student likes **green**

2. **Use the given information**  
   - \(P(B)=58\%\)  
   - \(P(G)=45\%\)  
   - \(P(B\cap G)=22\%\)

3. **Compute the probability that a student likes either blue or green**  
   \[
   P(B\cup G)=P(B)+P(G)-P(B\cap G)
   \]
   \[
   = 58\% + 45\% - 22\%
   = 103\% - 22\%
   = 81\%
   \]

4. **Find the complement**  
   The probability that a student likes *neither* color is the complement of “likes either color”:
   \[
   P(\text{neither}) = 1 - P(B\cup G) = 1 - 0.81 = 0.19
   \]

5. **Convert to a percentage**  
   Multiply by 100:
   \[
   0.19 \times 100\% = 19\%
   \]
