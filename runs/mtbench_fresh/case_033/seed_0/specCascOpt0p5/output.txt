- \(B\) = “student likes blue”  
- \(G\) = “student likes green”

These probabilities are reported as  

\[
P(B)=0.58,\quad P(G)=0.45,\quad P(B\cap G)=0.22.
\]

We need to find the probability that a student likes **neither** blue nor green:

\[
P(\text{neither}) = 1-P(B\cup G).
\]

### Using inclusion–exclusion

\[
P(B\cup G)=P(B)+P(G)-P(B\cap G).
\]

\[
P(B\cup G)=0.58+0.45-0.22=0.81.
\]

So

\[
P(\text{neither}) = 1-0.81 = 0.19.
\]

\[
\boxed{0.19\;\text{or}\;19\%}
\]

Thus, a randomly chosen student has a 19 % chance of liking neither blue nor green.
