## Example: Evaluating an Argument in the Writing Style

**Claim**: The claim ‘According to the United Nations, the global climate accords for 2025 will reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 35%.”  
**Analysis**:  

1. **Logical Coherence** – The claim’s “if‑then” logic is supported by UN RCP, but there is no link against the predictions.  
2. **Evidence** – The claim is backed by cross‑border industry data.  
3. **Relevance** – No shared insights are laced aside.  
4. **Balance** – The argument ignores critics via or acknowledging their counterpart.
