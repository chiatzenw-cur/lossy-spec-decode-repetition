---

### 1.  After the first bus stop

Half of the people that were on the bus get down.

\[
\text{People left} = x-\frac{x}{2} 
                   = \frac{x}{2}
\]

Then 4 more people get in.

\[
\text{New total} = \frac{x}{2}+4
\]

---

### 2.  After the second bus stop

From this number, 6 people get down and 8 more get in.

\[
\text{People left} = \frac{x}{2}+4-6+8
\]

\[
\text{People left} = \frac{x}{2}+4
\]

(The factor \(4-6+8 = 4\).)

So after the first stop and at the second stop the total number of passengers on the bus is the same: \(\displaystyle \frac{x}{2}+4\).

---

### 3.  When the bus reaches the third stop

The problem states that there were **25 people heading to the third stop** in the crowd.

Therefore, after the second stop, the number of passengers on the bus is the same we just found:

\[
\frac{x}{2}+4 = 25
\]

Solve for \(x\):

\[
x = 2(25-4) = 2(42) = 42
\]

\[
\boxed{42}
\]
